# Urban Customs Website Analysis

**Website:** [https://urbancustomsaz.com/](https://urbancustomsaz.com/)

## How the Website Operates

**Urban Customs** is a Phoenix, Arizona-based flooring contractor specializing in residential and commercial flooring sales, installation, design, repair, and refinishing. The company serves the entire Phoenix Valley (Phoenix, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria, Scottsdale, Surprise, and surrounding areas).

### Core Business Model & Services

- **Flooring Installation**: Hardwood, stone/natural stone (soapstone, slate, travertine, marble, granite), tile, bamboo (preferred Cali Bamboo installer), and engineered flooring.
- **Hardwood Floor Services**: Repair, restoration, refinishing, buffing/screening, sanding, recoating, and full replacement.
- **Design & Consultation**: Full design services that also extend into kitchens, bathrooms, cabinets, granite, and tile mosaics.
- **Key Differentiators**:
  - 20+ years of experience (family-run business)
  - Transferable lifetime workmanship warranty on installations
  - Emphasis on quality materials and clean, professional installation

### Website Structure & User Flow

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Homepage** | SEO-heavy landing page targeting local searches ("Flooring Contractors Phoenix", "Flooring Contractors Near Me", etc.). Highlights Design → Installation → Warranty pillars and pushes phone calls for estimates. |
| **About** | Builds trust with family history, experience, product range, and value propositions (best value, on-time, within budget). |
| **Services / Flooring Installation** | Detailed breakdowns of each flooring type, design process, installation approach, and warranties. Includes cost FAQs with third-party averages. |
| **Refinishing / Repair** | Problem-focused content covering water damage, scratches, termite damage, gaps, etc., with process explanations. |
| **Contact** | Simple form + phone (480-747-2516), text (480-381-9892), email, and physical address (by appointment only). |
| **FAQs & Specials** | Pricing transparency, no hidden fees, and limited-time promotional messaging. |

### How Leads Are Currently Generated

- Primarily **phone-driven** (strong emphasis on calling for estimates)
- Contact form submissions
- SEO-focused content that ranks for local flooring-related keywords
- Trust signals: experience claims, lifetime warranty, family business narrative

The site functions as a classic local service-business brochure + SEO magnet: attract search traffic → educate on services and approximate costs → build trust → convert mainly via phone or contact form.

---

## Recommendations to Increase Lead Generation

### 1. Strengthen Calls-to-Action (Highest Impact)

- Add prominent, persistent "**Get Free Estimate**", "**Schedule Free In-Home Consultation**", or "**Text for Quote**" buttons on every page (sticky header/footer + above-the-fold on mobile).
- Make phone and text numbers clickable (`tel:` and `sms:` links).
- Offer multiple low-commitment options instead of only "Call us."

### 2. Add Online Scheduling & Smarter Lead Capture

- Integrate a calendar booking tool (Calendly or similar) for free consultations.
- Create a simple multi-step quote form or cost estimator (square footage + flooring type → approximate range + "Request exact quote").
- Use exit-intent popups or slide-ins offering a free "Phoenix Flooring Buyer's Guide" or special offer in exchange for contact information.

### 3. Improve Visual Proof & Trust Signals

- Build a strong project gallery with before/after photos, captions (location, flooring type, timeline).
- Embed Google/Yelp reviews and star ratings.
- Add video testimonials and short case studies.
- Visually highlight the mobile showroom and lifetime warranty with badges/icons.

### 4. Activate the Specials Page

- Make current specials specific and time-sensitive (e.g., "15% off hardwood installation this month" or limited financing).
- Promote specials site-wide with banners or countdown timers.
- Offer limited free add-ons (e.g., free consultation + material samples).

### 5. Mobile-First Optimization

- Ensure fast load times and large, easy-to-tap buttons.
- Prioritize click-to-call and simplified forms on mobile (most local service searches happen on phones).

### 6. Content & SEO Upgrades

- Expand FAQs with more Phoenix-specific advice (climate considerations, maintenance tips).
- Add a blog/resources section ("Best Flooring for Arizona Heat", cost guides, etc.).
- Implement schema markup for LocalBusiness, Services, and FAQs to improve search visibility.

### 7. Live Chat / Chatbot + Retargeting

- Add a chatbot that answers common questions and captures leads, or live chat during business hours.
- Run basic retargeting ads (Google/Facebook) to visitors who viewed installation or refinishing pages but didn't convert.

### 8. Measurement & Testing

- Set up proper analytics (form submissions, call tracking with unique numbers, heatmaps).
- A/B test headlines, CTA wording, form length, and offers.

---

## Summary

The current website has a solid local SEO foundation and clear service messaging. The biggest opportunity lies in reducing friction and giving visitors more ways to take the next step (especially free consultations and online scheduling) while strengthening social proof and urgency. Implementing the recommendations above—particularly stronger CTAs, online booking, and better visual proof—should significantly increase qualified lead volume.
